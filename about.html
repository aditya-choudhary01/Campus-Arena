About CampusArena

A student-run esports desk for campus cups, squad finding, and a shared rulebook across BGMI, Valorant, CS2, and EA Sports FC.

What we do

CampusArena lists free college tournaments, helps players fill open roles, and keeps match rules in one place so clubs do not rewrite the same brief every week.

Who can play

Currently enrolled students with a college email (.edu or .ac.in). Alumni and staff can organize events, but they cannot sit on a playing roster.

How to start

Create an account, browse the calendar, register a team, or post a teammate request if you still need a role filled.

Questions

Use the contact form for protests, prize questions, or club partnerships. Organizers read saved messages from the JSON file on the local server.

Join the next campus cup

Register a roster or message the desk if your club wants to host.
