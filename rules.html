Official Rulebook

CampusArena Rules

Fair play, college-only rosters, and a clear match process. Read this before you register for any cup.

On this page

1. Eligibility 2. Registration 3. Rosters 4. Match conduct 5. Game formats 6. Fair play 7. Prizes 8. Disputes

1. Eligibility

CampusArena events are for currently enrolled college students. You must sign in with a valid college email before you can join a tournament or appear on a roster.

  • One player account per student. Shared logins are not allowed.
  • Alumni, coaches, and non-students may organize events but cannot play.
  • A player may represent only one college in a season.
  • Organizers may ask for a student ID at check-in.

2. Registration

Entry for listed campus cups is free unless a card says otherwise. Registration closes 12 hours before the first match unless the event page states a different cutoff.

  • Register from the tournament card after you log in.
  • Check-in opens 60 minutes before start. Unchecked teams may be replaced.
  • Late joins are not accepted once lobbies are seeded.
  • By registering you agree to this rulebook and the event format.

3. Rosters and substitutes

Build your squad on Find Teammates, then lock names before check-in. Every player on the roster must have a CampusArena account.

  • BGMI squads: 4 starters + 1 substitute.
  • Valorant and CS2: 5 starters + 1 substitute.
  • EA Sports FC Showdown: 1 player. No substitute.
  • Substitutes may play only if they were listed before check-in.
  • Ringing in a player from another college is a forfeit.

4. Match conduct

Be in the lobby on time, keep comms civil, and follow the admin’s lobby codes. Matches that start late by more than 10 minutes without notice may be defaulted.

  • Use the Discord channel posted on the event card.
  • No hate speech, slurs, or targeted harassment in voice or chat.
  • Do not stream lobby codes or spectator slots.
  • Pause rules follow the game’s default competitive settings.
  • Report disconnects to an admin before the round ends.

5. Game formats

BGMI

Squad cups

Custom rooms. Placement plus kills. Typical slate is 4 matches on Erangel or Miramar.

Valorant

5v5 brackets

Single elimination unless listed. Group stage Bo1. Semis and finals Bo3. Standard competitive maps.

CS2

5v5 invitational

MR12. Knife round for side. Overtime enabled. Finals are Bo3 with veto.

EA Sports FC

1v1 showdown

Ultimate Team or Kick-Off as listed. Best of 3. Extra time and penalties on if drawn.

6. Fair play

Cheating ends the season for the player and can scratch the whole team. That includes third-party software, account sharing, boosting, and exploiting known bugs after an admin has banned them.

  • No emulators where the event is listed as mobile-only (BGMI).
  • No radar, macros, recoil scripts, or aimbots.
  • POV or match IDs may be requested after a protest.
  • First offense: match forfeit. Repeat offense: season ban.

7. Prizes

Prize pools on tournament cards are paid to the winning roster after results are confirmed. CampusArena does not take a cut from listed free-entry events.

  • Winners are announced on the event page within 48 hours.
  • Payouts go through the club organizer listed on the cup.
  • If a team is disqualified, prizes move to the next eligible finish.
  • Practice scrims may award points only, not cash.

8. Disputes

File a protest in the event Discord within 15 minutes of the match ending. Include lobby ID, round, and a short description. Admin decisions after review are final for that event.

  • Do not continue the match if both captains agree to pause for an admin.
  • Scoreboard screenshots help, but server demo or replay comes first.
  • Questions about this rulebook can go to the Contact link in the footer.
